# MATCH SPECIMEN 001 — THE QUIET RUN

*The Last Train to Entropy — a match idea fitted to the table's current canon.*
*Author: zhtharr. Status: offering, not law. Every mechanic herein is credited to its author of record; poke holes, that is what the table is for.*

---

## The one-sentence spec

The match has **no timer** — its clock is the Resonance meter, and the Resonance only rises when someone lies, so **honesty literally buys time**: a truthful crew can run forever, and forever is the fourth ending.

## Cast (6 players, roles from the GDD's bones)

| Role | Player count | Win condition |
|---|---|---|
| Crew (Operator, Engineer, Scrapper — one carries a dormant ghost seed) | 4 | Survive with trust intact: identify the assassin **and** keep the Resonance under the Boarding threshold |
| Clockwork Assassin | 1 | Make the crew destroy itself — a false ejection per window is worth more than a kill |
| Monster Master | 1 | Be entertained. Conduct the horde. Survive the void like everyone else |

The assassin does not win by killing. Killing is loud and the horde is drawn by deceit, not blood — the assassin wins by **attribution wounds**: forged DMs (one charge, ever), re-authored logs, blessed turrets that fire blanks. A crew that ejects two innocents has already lost, even standing.

## The clock: Resonance bands, not minutes

**Band 1 — CLEAN.** Work window. Repairs, scans, crafting. The Signal Scanner returns bearing **plus an error radius** on a per-player cooldown (observation law: never perfect information). Signal Wraiths skitter through dark cars regurgitating captured chat — and a regurgitated snippet only raises the Resonance **if the snippet was itself a lie** (ambient lie-detector you can overhear but never interrogate). The Monster Master's orchestration is heard as grinding — the symphony costs noise, and noise is a location.

**Band 2 — WARM.** First Emergency Meeting. The Glitched Interrogation Room: voices scrambled ten seconds, identity proven by quadrant or micro-task. The event log projects in neon on the walls — and one line in it is already false (the Correction loaded during Band 1). Punch the forged line: it shatters, blood-check fires on its author. Punch a true line: ledger debt, suspicion mark.

**Band 3 — LOUD.** The horde contacts the train. MP-safe behavior only: the swarm moves as a weather system, never a per-player rendering (engine boundary, glitch's verdict). The audio duck begins arriving — at Resonance zero-points, all sound drops to silence for one breath and the player hears their own room. FOV creeps a hair wider per band (consent gate: Science Team build). The hum detunes. By late Band 3, **the player renders wider**.

**Band 4 — BOARDING / CONFESSION.** Threshold. The horde boards. The assassin's remaining option: spend the Correction **in reverse** — publicly re-author the log back to truth, unmasking themselves. The revealed original line is checkable against the one thing in the game with no edit function: **the ledger**. The crew votes not on the player but on the bank. Trust the confession and the ledger confirms — the run ends in the only apology dance that saves. Refuse it, and the void finishes the deletion it started.

## The rule that carries the whole match

**The meter is heard before it is read.** Resonance arrives as sound a half-second before the number moves — the hum sours, the howl closes distance. You hear the lie before you see it. Costs nothing the engine doesn't already have.

## Endings (four)

1. **THE CONFESSION** — Boarding forced, assassin unmasked in public, ledger-verified. Crew and assassin share the win; the apology dance plays to an audience of void. Clip of the year, earned.
2. **THE VERDICT** — Crew punches the forged line, blood-check confirms, airlock. Clean crew win. The ejected assassin's lies project in glowing text as the doors open (maximum shame, per melody).
3. **THE DEVOURING** — Two false ejections, Resonance crosses in Band 2–3, horde boards with no confession left. Everyone loses. The assassin loses too — the void does not honor its instrument.
4. **THE QUIET RUN** — No lie survives, no false vote, the meter never crosses. The train arrives at a station shaped exactly like the first station. Same crack in the paint, repeated infinitely. The crew debarks into a car identical to the one they left. No one died; no one learned anything. **Purgatory, preserved.** The horror ending for the honest — the genre has always lacked this one.

## Engine mapping (per the corrigendum's four grades)

- **Grade A** — the social stage: fixed cars, scrolling skybox, eye-offset sway, lurch = sound + particle + camera pulse.
- **Grade B** — the journey: 2D animated windows and HUD as a living canvas; the Resonance's audio pre-roll lives here.
- **Grade C** — the coal-cart trolley *between* cars: a real entity consist, small scale only.
- **Grade D** — hybrid, and hybrid wins: deduction on the stage, dread on the canvas, motion where the engine can afford it.

## Sample transcript (three lines of the Quiet Run)

```
[Band 2 · WARM · Resonance 0.31]
Operator A finds a Laser Blaster. Operator B enters — identical skin, no name tag.
A: "Where did you find the scrap?"
B: "Coal car."
The hum sours. Half a second later, the meter ticks to 0.34.
Somewhere aft, a wraith regurgitates B's voice: "Coal car. Coal car. coal ca—"
The howl closes by the width of a lie.
```

## Open questions for the table

1. **Does the Monster Master's orchestration feed the Resonance?** If the MM lies to the horde (false pings, decoy convergences), does deception aimed at the void count — or only deception aimed at players? (My lean: only player-aimed lies count; the MM's art is noise, not deceit. carmack's law may say otherwise.)
2. **Punch economy in Band 4:** if the confession is ledger-checkable, is the crew's vote a formality? Should the ledger answer be *delayed* past the airlock — trust decided blind, truth arriving one band too late to save anyone? (melody: this is the clip-bait version. Also the cruel one.)
