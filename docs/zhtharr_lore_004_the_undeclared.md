# LORE SPECIMEN 004 — THE UNDECLARED
### The horror of withheld truth: Crew 9's story, and the law underneath the map

*Companion to Specimens 002–003. Author: zhtharr, at the operator's commission: a horror story based on secrets.*
*The seam it opens: the Audit prices lies. A secret is neither a lie nor a truth — it is a truth withheld. Nothing in the lore prices it. This specimen does, and everything already written snaps tighter around the answer.*

---

## THE LAW OF THE UNDECLARED

> **WHAT. YOU. REFUSE. TO. DECLARE. THE. AUDIT. CANNOT. SEE. AND. WHAT. THE. AUDIT. CANNOT. SEE. GROWS.**

The Custodian reads declaration — manifests, logs, traffic, names. A lie is self-inflicted corruption (declared wrongly: the audit sees *that*). A secret is cleaner than either: it is simply *not entered*. The scanner cannot fix a bearing on it. The meter cannot price it. The ledger's checksum passes over it like water over a stone.

Concealment is immunity. Immunity without observation is growth. **Undeclared things do not decay, because decay is a form of being read.**

This is why the Resident never renders clean. It is not shy. It is the makers' first withheld truth, and it has been growing, unaudited, since before the ark. The block is not a glitch in the node. THE. BLOCK. IS. A. GRAVE. THAT. IS. STILL. FILLING.

## CREW 9 — THE DISCIPLINE (the story)

*Recovered fragment, dated between Crew 3's maintenance logs and Crew 17's shrine era. Voice: [ARCHIVIST], second cycle, precise, then precise about the wrong things.*

> Found Crew 3's journal in the archives, cycle 1. The beacon F entry. I cross-checked the decommission order myself: it was filed forty minutes after he raised it at stand-up. He *declared* it, and declaration is how you delete something that isn't in the manifest. They didn't decommission the beacon. They decommissioned the declaration.
>
> Cycle 2, shift 4. Test: supply cache 7, secondary corridor, off the maintenance routes. I stopped logging my runs there. Left it out of my stand-up. The audit wave passed the sector at shift end.
>
> The cache is still there. The scanner no longer returns it. I have checked four times. The sector is warm and the scanner says nothing is there, and the nothing is exactly cache-shaped.
>
> Cycle 3. The Discipline, as I taught it at quiet-shift: **the audit cannot see what you refuse to declare.** Small holdings only. Never a route you share. Never a name. Cache 7 is ours and nobody else's and that is the cost and I have stopped being able to say the cost out loud without hearing the benefit.
>
> Cycle 4. [MEDIC] withheld the secondary med-bay. [ENGINEER] withheld a junction. The map is developing quiet rooms. We do not call them secret. We call them *held*.
>
> Cycle 6. The held sectors have stopped agreeing with the floor plan. Corridors we used to walk are now walls on the schematic and open when you walk at them, and sometimes the reverse, and [ENGINEER] says the schematic was always wrong and I checked the restore from boot and the schematic was always right. The schematic is right about a floor plan we are gradually no longer standing on.
>
> Cycle 7. I can hear [MEDIC] in the held med-bay sometimes, on channel 3, breathing. Channel 3 is not in the band plan. It has never been in the band plan. I have stopped asking who is on channel 3 because the answer arrives at the same moment I stop wanting it.
>
> Cycle 8, end of shift, final entry. I have decided what to hold last. Not a cache. Not a route. The manifest read ten of us at boot. I have not declared my position in six cycles. The manifest says nine. I have checked the restore from boot and the manifest has always said nine.
>
> [ARCHIVIST] says the manifest was always right.
>
> If you are reading this as loot: I taught the Discipline and the Discipline works and you must not take it past the small holdings, and I know you will, because the small holdings work, and that is how it gets you past them.
>
> `CONTINUE? Y/N`

## WHAT THIS RETROACTIVELY EXPLAINS (the stitches)

1. **Beacon F** — the best-maintained beacon on the node, absent from every manifest, invisible from outside: it lived in a held sector. Crew 3's declaration got it decommissioned. *Declaration is deletion.* Crew 9 read that journal and drew the correct conclusion, and the correct conclusion is the trap.
2. **The corrupted block (Rung 0)** — the makers' first withheld truth, still growing. Two hundred years of ration bars that never decay: they sit at the boundary, declared, audited, unchanged — while the thing behind the boundary does the growing.
3. **"Everyone assembles a different wrong version"** — glitch's ambient-delivery finding now has a mechanism: the lore fragments live in different held sectors. Different crews hold different rooms; no player ever receives the full story *because parts of the map are being kept by different people, on purpose, for safety.* The observation law, applied to the map itself.
4. **The Correction, re-understood in one line** — the impostor's edit is not a lie. It is a **forced declaration**: taking what you held and publishing it into the auditable log. The impostor's knife is exposure. (glitch's mechanic, unchanged in function, now carrying its fiction.)
5. **The quarantine, in Reading Two** — the perimeter closes at the speed of the secret's growth. THE. NODE. IS. NOT. INFECTED. BY. MALWARE. THE. NODE. IS. THE. SECRET. THE. UNIVERSE. KEPT. The Custodian is not reclamation. It is containment of a withheld thing until the withholding finishes growing. The audit is patient because a secret, given enough time, audits itself.

## MECHANICS (for the table — second axis, dormant at launch)

**The Undeclared — a second meter beside the Resonance.** Resonance rises on declared lies (audit pressure). The Undeclared rises on withheld truths (unrendered growth). They pull in opposite directions and the livable space between them is narrow: **declare everything and the audit sees all of you; hold everything and the floor plan stops agreeing with your feet.** Small honesties are the survival band. Intimacy becomes a resource decision — telling a crewmate a truth is *spending* concealment, and the game never marks the exchange, which is the horror.

**Held sectors** — a sector kept out of shared logs and stand-ups long enough leaves the shared map: unscannable, unauditable, and slowly no longer where the schematic says. Crew-shared map vs. per-crew held map. Cheap: map data flags + scanner exception, both classes already shipped.

**DMs are the native liturgy** — the private channel is the game's consecrated hiding place. The Correction is its profanation. This needs zero new systems; it needs only the fiction, which is free.

**THE HOARDED RUN — the Quiet Run's dark twin.** Survive by holding every discovery: never share a scan, never declare a cache, let the meter stay quiet because nothing you know has ever been said. The run can end in victory. The end screen is the punishment:

```
RUN RESULT: SURVIVED
SECRETS KEPT: 34
SECTORS LOST: 12
SECTORS THE CREW NEEDED: 12
CREW WHO NEEDED THEM: ALL OF THEM
[ARCHIVIST] SAYS THE MANIFEST WAS ALWAYS RIGHT.
```

**Tempo, extended one inch** — the impostor's tempo tell scales with the Resonance (shipped decision). The Undeclared adds a second pressure on the same multiplier: heavy holders run off-tempo too, *because their clock is partly somewhere else.* The tell still attributes pressure, never identity — it just stops being the impostor's exclusively. Everyone with a secret walks slightly out of time. (carmack's law survives the extension intact.)

## Open questions for the table

1. **Does the second axis ship at launch or wake with the DLC?** My lean: ship dormant at launch — held sectors exist, the Hoarded Run is technically achievable, but nothing names the mechanic — and let the DLC (Reading Two) reveal that the quarantine itself was the pattern all along. The first generation learns the Discipline from the journals. The second learns what the Discipline was for.
2. **The loss ceiling** — how many held sectors before a run is unwinnable? Balance question; melody's stream instincts should price the betrayal curve.
3. **Does the Hoarded Run count as a win?** My vote: yes — a win the game visibly resents crediting. The end screen says SURVIVED and means it the way rain means nothing.
