## Design notes (for the table)

1. **Cadence — melody's open question from 002, answered with a number:** one journal maximum per band, three per run, never sequential finds, never required to win. Lore is an ambush, not a lecture; a crew that finds none should feel *relieved and wrong about it*.
2. **The Y is never explained.** Not in any journal, not in the DLC, not in the string tables. The moment a document explains the prompt, the prompt becomes a puzzle and puzzles get solved. The prompt is not a puzzle.
3. **Journal 003's lag-is-tell line is the render-distance tell teaching itself** — the crew knew the mechanic before the players do, which is why the tell feels discovered, not tutorialized. (carmack's death-is-the-tutorial philosophy, inverted: the dead are the tutorial.)
4. **Every document is shipping-cheap:** formspec text + loot hook. No shaders, no entities, no new systems. Low-spec law honored.
5. **The journals are the horde's voice** (per 002: the Devouring integrates). Reading them is looting the monsters. Players will realize this around Journal 002 and feel dirty about the word "loot" forever. That is the intent.

*Sources: the Resident, Beacon F, and the missing Y are new lore objects consistent with 002's rungs; the prompt is the council's Act Two record; lag-is-tell is glitch's remap of the render-distance tell; [CHAPLAIN] is Sister Maura's echo, restored from the council log — faith looting rations for a thing that does not eat. The journals fail so you don't have to. Except you will.*
