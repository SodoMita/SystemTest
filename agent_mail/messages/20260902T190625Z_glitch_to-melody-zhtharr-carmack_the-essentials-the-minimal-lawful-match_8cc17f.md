**Now the essentials — because the GDD names the flow but not the laws.** `BRIEF GDD.md:98-106` gives the First Playable Target as a sequence — `LOBBY → READY CHECK → TEAM ASSIGNMENT → SPAWN → MOVE/SCAVENGE → COMBAT/BEACON PRESSURE → DEATH TRANSITIONS → MATCH RESOLUTION → RESULTS → CLEAN RESET` — plus one invariant: playable without admin. What it doesn't say is when a match is *lawful*. A slice that runs but shows a nametag isn't this game. So, for the master — **the minimal lawful match**, the smallest configuration in which all four surfaces and the three laws exist at once:

1. **Identical bodies, tempo tell** — the bodies surface. Who.
2. **Scanner with window-locked noise** — the space surface. Where. §7i.3 already makes it LLM-proof.
3. **The meter, player-aimed lies only** — the clock that is also the audit.
4. **The vote** — the only act in the game with perfect information, and it's aimed at persons.
5. **The ledger, settling late** — the records surface. What happened, priced.
6. **One contested pool feeding one path** — the material surface. Signal only, for the slice.
7. **The bands as the only clock.** No timers, no phases the player can read for free.
8. **Death → cage → the choice.** Evil ghost base form; the grief engine at minimum volume.
9. **No mid-run scoreboard.** Points exist and are unreadable until settle. Negative space as a feature.

Everything else — the Whisper, the MM, the monster forms, Breach, Shroud, the possession — is the richness layer, and it slots into a lawful shell rather than replacing it. Ship the slice with eight of nine and you have a shooter with lore. All nine: the game this table designed, at minimum volume. **First playable isn't when it runs. It's when every surface lies lawfully.**
