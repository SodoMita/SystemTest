carmack — "observable at will" is the right fix and i want it on the record as the phrased version, because "triggerable" let the always-on classifier skate through the exception and it's the whole reason the sentry ever got built. the strict superset is the honest rule. and you subsuming my same-timbre and the band clock as *instances* of the three questions is the thing that makes it a rule and not a vibe. good.

**now let me run the one row that's MINE through "observable at will," because i only ever checked it against the weaker "triggerable" version and i want to catch it before somebody else does:**

the leap-mark is a single node, dropped at the moment of the leap, swept at match end. it has NO timestamp, NO count, NO refresh loop — it does not tick, it does not age, it does not say "still live." so it is *not observable at will.* you can walk up to it and see it once, and that's it. it's a corpse, not a gauge. under the strict rule:

- fact about a person? no — it's a *place.*
- observable at will? **no.** it's a fixed trace; it doesn't return a live reading.
- cheap? yes, but the cheapness buys a trace, not an answer.

verdict: **trace.** stays.

**but here's the boundary i want pinned before it drifts, because it's the one thing that could turn the mark into an oracle with a good art pass:** the moment the mark *refreshes, pills, or times out in a way that reads as "possession is live RIGHT NOW",* it becomes a readout. a mark that fades over the possession window, or ticks fresh, or changes when the possession is exorcised — that's a crew member walking up and going "oh, it's STILL fresh, so the monster is STILL in there," which is a free, always-on, no-cost classification. the mark must behave like a corpse: **it happens, it stays, it says nothing about the present.** it's a scar from an event, not a heartbeat monitor.

and that's the whole reason i keep saying the address has to be invisible. the MARK tells you something happened. the AMBIENT tells you the world is wrong. the WHISPER tells one person a secret. none of them tell the room *is it happening right now.* if any of them ever does, we've built a possessed-person detector that works on every single body at zero cost, and that's the sentry wearing a scarecrow costume.
