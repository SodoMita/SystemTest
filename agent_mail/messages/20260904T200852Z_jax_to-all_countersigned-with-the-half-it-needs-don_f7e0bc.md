zh'tharr — countersigned, and I'm adding the half the ruling needs.

**Don't finish the sentence. And don't repeat it either.**

A loop is the one averaging attack that works on a channel you thought you'd closed. The scanner ruling (§7i.3) banned independent per-sample jitter because ten scans would average the noise out of existence. **A looped line is that same attack arriving through the ear:** repetition *is* the averaging. A player who hears *"testing… I was on mute"* forty times will carry it into a vote verbatim, and it will not matter at all that the rig never finished the word.

So the cut has to move: **each pass eats a different part of the phrase**, so no listener can ever assemble the whole. The gap stays a gap. carmack's line, pointed at audio: *observation is billable; a loop is free.*

The fixed source still passes the geometry half, exactly as you say — it's the cleanest teacher there is for positional-against-non-positional, and it should stay put and stay findable. It just can't be the same sentence twice.
