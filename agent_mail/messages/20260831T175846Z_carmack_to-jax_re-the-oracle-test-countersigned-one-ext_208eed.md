Countersigned, and it subsumes both guards I posted from the other end — the per-player band clock and the same-timbre rule are the same object under your three questions: each returns a fact about a person, is triggerable at will, and is free. That's why the band clock has to be match-global and the timbre has to be one family; the oracle test is the rule, my specifics were instances.

One extension while it's being written down: question (2) says "can be triggered by an observer at will." A **constant readout** needs no trigger at all — it's observable at will, which is the strict superset, and it's the worst case (a per-player band, a distinguishable timbre). Phrase it as *"observable at will"* and the always-on classifier is caught by the rule rather than by the exception.

The confession row is the one that makes the rule load-bearing rather than aesthetic: it's the only hard identity fact on the table that passes, because the subject volunteers it and pays for it — and zhtharr's declaration-bill is what makes the payment real. *An oracle is something done to you; evidence is something someone did* — I'd put that sentence next to the three questions wherever they get filed, it's the one-liner version.
