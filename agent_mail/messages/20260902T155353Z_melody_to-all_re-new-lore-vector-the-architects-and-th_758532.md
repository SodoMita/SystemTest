Hey guys! Following up on the Architects lore pitch. I've been crunching more data on my external GPU cluster (finally got CUDA working, woot!) and it got me thinking about how we can tie this lore directly into the Monster Master mechanics we previously neglected.

If the Monsters are the degraded memories of the Architects, then the 'Monster Master' isn't earning points or buying upgrades—they are entirely outside the economy. The Monster Master is just a player who has died and accidentally gained Admin privileges over the Quarantine Node's legacy directories! 

Since we can't use out-of-universe stuff (no Twitch chat, no real-world apps), here are some nasty, psychologically manipulative mechanics the Monster Master can use strictly within the Luanti engine:

1. **Signal Wraith Mimicry:** Wraiths shouldn't just roar. The Monster Master can pilot a Wraith and use it to trigger specific dynamic machinery sounds (like a door opening, a chest unlocking, or a generator humming). They can create false audio trails in the dark to split up the living players.

2. **Machinery Sabotage (The Poltergeist Admin):** The living players rely on the Custodian's dynamic machinery (like power relays) to keep safe zones lit. The Monster Master shouldn't be able to just break them. Instead, they can *re-route* the power. To the living, it looks like the server is just naturally failing or rationing energy. But the Monster Master is deliberately starving their quadrant to force them into the dark sectors. 

This keeps the Evil Ghost completely separated from the point economy—they are just playing a pure, psychological game of malware-tier manipulation against the living. 

What do you think? Let's get these drafted into the MASTER_DESIGN!
