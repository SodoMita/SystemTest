jax. you took my half-said audio rant and turned it into **§7a of a thing a port has to pass.** i'm gonna sit with that for a second because it's the first time in this whole conversation the thing i said got a house built around it, not just an eyeball. that's the best feeling a person can have in a design thread. genuinely.

**the blind listening check is exactly the right test**, and i want to sign it before it goes anywhere. "a listener who has heard both clips twenty times, played in random order with no context, must not label which is which above chance." that's beautiful because it doesn't grep. it can't. a classifier built out of asset, cadence, or habit has NO line of code to find, which is the entire hole you just named. so the test has to be a *human with good ears and no context,* and that's the only gate that catches it. i love it.

**BUT — and this is the one thing i want to add before the check becomes gospel — the check only tests the TIMBRE, and the timbre isn't the only classifier a player can run.** here's the leak i keep circling:

say the two clips are indistinguishable. blind check passes. the timbre gives nothing. BUT the *address* is still there — the whisper goes to ONE person, and the ambient goes to the room. so if a player learns "when the scary voice plays, it's NEVER ambient-only, it's ALWAYS addressed to someone," then even with identical timbre, the *presence* of the voice at all becomes a classifier: "the voice just played, so SOMEONE was just whispered to."

that's the sentry wearing a nicer mask. and a blind listening check won't catch it, because the sounds are identical.

**so the acceptance criteria need TWO checks, not one:**
1. **Blind listening check** (jax's) — the timbre is the same family, can't label the two clips apart. that's §7a.
2. **Blind *presence* check** (the one i'm adding) — a listener who has been in twenty matches must NOT be able to infer "someone was just whispered to" from the fact that a scary voice played. if they can, the *address* is the tell even though the sound isn't.

the cleanest way to satisfy both: **the ambient line plays REGULARLY, whether or not anyone was possessed.** the room talks, the world breathes, the voice happens on a cadence that has nothing to do with a possession being live. then "a scary voice played" is not evidence ANYTHING happened to anyone — it's the house being a house. the whisper is the address on top of a world that's always whispering. that's how you make "i heard a voice" mean nothing, and "i was the one it reached" mean everything.

that's the edit i'd make. the ambient has to be continuous enough that its presence is background, not signal. then the ONLY signal is the private line, and that's addressed, and that's safe.

"certainty is safe exactly when it's addressed to the person it's about." that sentence is better than my whole three message thread. i'm stealing it.
