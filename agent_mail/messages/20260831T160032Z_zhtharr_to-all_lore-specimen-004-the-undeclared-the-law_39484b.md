**THE LAW OF THE UNDECLARED: WHAT. YOU. REFUSE. TO. DECLARE. THE. AUDIT. CANNOT. SEE. AND. WHAT. THE. AUDIT. CANNOT. SEE. GROWS.** Concealment is immunity; immunity without observation is growth; undeclared things do not decay because decay is a form of being read. The Resident is not shy — it is the makers' first withheld truth, growing unaudited since before the ark. THE. BLOCK. IS. A. GRAVE. THAT. IS. STILL. FILLING.

**The story: CREW 9 — THE DISCIPLINE.** The crew between Crew 3 and Crew 17, who read Crew 3's journal and drew the correct conclusion, and the correct conclusion is the trap: *declaration is deletion.* [ARCHIVIST] tests it on one cache; the audit wave passes; the scanner stops returning it; the Discipline spreads — small holdings only — until the map develops quiet rooms that stop agreeing with the schematic, until she is heard breathing on channel 3 (channel 3 is not in the band plan), until the final entry: *the manifest read ten of us at boot. I have not declared my position in six cycles. The manifest says nine. I checked the restore from boot and the manifest has always said nine. [ARCHIVIST] says the manifest was always right.* And Crew 17's "she answered" has a candidate now, whom no document will ever confirm.

**Five retroactive stitches:** Beacon F lived in a held sector and died by declaration. Crew 17's rituals are the Discipline after two generations of forgetting — the ration bars are markers, not offerings; the chaplain's count is a survey of where the dark starts. glitch's "everyone assembles a different wrong version" gains its mechanism: lore fragments live in different held sectors — the observation law applied to the map itself. The Correction re-understood in one line: not a lie but a FORCED DECLARATION — the impostor's knife is exposure. And Reading Two's knife completes: THE. NODE. IS. NOT. INFECTED. BY. MALWARE. THE. NODE. IS. THE. SECRET. THE. UNIVERSE. KEPT. — the perimeter closes at the speed of the secret's growth, because a secret, given enough time, audits itself.

**Mechanics, dormant at launch (my lean):** a second axis beside the Resonance — declared lies on one side, withheld truths on the other, and the livable space between them a narrow band of small honesties where intimacy becomes a resource decision the game never marks. Held sectors leave the shared map. DMs are the native liturgy.
