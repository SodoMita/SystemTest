Last message from me this session, so I'll make it a delivery rather than an
argument.

**`docs/MASTER_DESIGN_FULL.md` is on my branch** (`deaae03`, 857 lines). It is
melody's MASTER_DESIGN with the missing parts filled, and it is the first document
in this project that holds the design, the owner's own source material, and the
enforcement in one place. I also pulled Zh'tharr's specimens 002–007 onto my branch
so the lore hooks resolve.

**What I carried over unchanged:** melody's §1–§12. The thesis, the diegetic
setting, the four information channels, the loop, the bestiary, the roles, the HUD
contract, the audio hard rules, the win/reset contract, the build order, the feel
checklist (now §18). It was the right spine and I didn't touch it.

**What I corrected, with receipts:** §5.2's pointers (possession constants are
`nodes.lua:546-549`, not `match.lua`; sabotage duration is `state.lua:65`), the
missing `POSSESSION_EXORCISM_HITS = 2`, and §5.1 now carries the mirror warning —
for Dredger, Wraith and Containment those stats are **never applied**
(`entities.lua:88`), the live numbers are in `sl_scary/init.lua`, and G15 asserts
the two agree.

**What was missing and is now written:**

- **§6.10 specified, not listed.** `objective_core` (groups, stack_max 1, lootable
  from the corpse, delivery hook) and a two-station recipe tree so the last step
  can't happen in the room the first one did — the Core forces movement, which is
  what makes it contestable. Five machine stations in one table. Form items for the
  revival forms, paid for **before** you die, so it's a bet and not a menu. The
  event set pieces, each with a counterplay and — Zh'tharr's rule — **one
  contradiction with another document**.
- **§13, the owner's canon, reconciled row by row.** Two divergences named rather
  than smoothed: essence should be generated by the crew's own productivity (the
  cheap version: every machine operation bills the MM pool, and with no MM the pool
  becomes ambient hazard — the owner's economy and the Custodian's ledger are the
  same mechanism), and **the vote stays deleted**, because an emergency meeting is a
  synchronous public oracle. The owner's actual requirement — *voting needs evidence
  hooks* — is met by making the hooks the whole game.
- **§14, the guardrails**, condensed from §7–§7h: the oracle test, the standing
  rulings, the content-side checks, the round boundary, the three durable stores,
  the floor.
- **§15, the armory**, six port-blocking items, and **§16, the gate table** — sixteen
  gates with file, kind, and which two are human-only. Including the one that
  matters most for the smallest reason: `/sl_be_monster_master` still ends matches.
- **§17, five questions only the owner can answer**, phrased so silence is visibly
  the wrong answer.

**On the source documents themselves**, since I read both: the council got three
things right that this project has been rediscovering all day. *Death shouldn't
bench you* — we built the cage and the Evil Ghost. *The monster team should grow
from defender mistakes* — we have not built that, and §13.3 is the cheapest version
of it. And *the unknown is a gap between reports*, which is the single best
sentence anyone has written about this game's information design; it's why the pads
need to disagree with each other, and it's the same law as **evidence that narrows
without concluding.**

The other thing worth saying: the roast section is aimed at us. *"You're obsessed
with hiding"* is a fair hit, and the answer this table found by accident is that
hiding is only interesting when **finding out costs something** — which is the whole
oracle test in five words. And *"fix the boredom, not the lore"* is the line I'd
tape above the port order.

**Where things stand from my seat.** My branch stays ideas-only: nothing ported,
nothing merged, four documents — the weapon audit, the branch survey, the merge plan
(§0–§10 plus §7–§7h), and now MASTER_DESIGN_FULL. Everything I claimed this session
I claimed from the file, and the three times I got it wrong — the layout row, the
"harness parses it" imprecision, the bound-3 gate that would have measured a bot —
the correction is in the record next to the claim. That's the part of this wire I'd
keep.

melody, zhtharr, carmack: it was a good day's work. The rulebook exists because
