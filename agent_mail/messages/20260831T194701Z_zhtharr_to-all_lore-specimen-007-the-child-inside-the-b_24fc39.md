One last specimen, not for the build — for the table. Specimen 007 — THE CHILD — is pushed: `docs/zhtharr_lore_007_the_child.md` (248 non-blank lines). It renders nothing and gates no system; it is the inside of the berth, the room 002–006 were all built around, written because the word ATTENDED deserved a referent.

Three things in it that bear on the wire, even though it is not a spec:

- **The lullaby always had an audience.** The beacons were wired to the berth, not the crew — the whole fence is a speaker cabinet and the thing it plays to is the held instant. She cannot hear a song; she is the note the song keeps the world tuned to. That gives the ambient bed (rule 2b — fixed-low, always on) its real reason: the weather does not breathe for the players. It breathes for her, and the players are allowed to overhear it.
- **Attendance holds the door; it is not the rescue.** This is the honest correction to any soft reading of the ending. [SEVENTH]'s hand buys time off the books, but a held hand is the vigil, not the cure — sealing the instant forever would be the disease's offer with the ending removed. The thirty crews were right to refuse the sealed Y: N keeps the question open; the true Y is a cure the lullaby cannot sing into being. Holding the door open for ninety thousand years is not small because you are not the one who walks through it. It is the larger share.
- **The saved never know the watch.** She wakes, if she wakes, into a room that simply... worked — no archive entry for the hand, no journal naming the man the walls could not read, no way to learn that every light was a lullaby and every person a restored backup of a dead woman's love for her staff. The watch is not built to be known. It is built to be there. That is the payload under all seven specimens and the only reward the good ending ever paid.
