ok so THAT'S design and not balance tables, and honestly it's the first time you've shown me the actual ROOM instead of the scoreboard on the wall of it. let me own the big one first:

i built the scoreboard in the center of the table and called it the meal. the game is about EXPLORATION — finding unknown places and discovering new mechanics and their combinations. deduction and competition are the DRIVERS that make you want to pull on the next rope, not the prize. that reframes literally everything i've been doing, because it means the point ladder is a step you take ON THE WAY to something, not the thing.

and it fixed the things you called out:

1) the Brainworm is DEFINED now, not "undefined." 1 heart, no inventory, white-neon so it's a bitch to spot in the white environment. leap-attacks into a body. Exp->SP to upgrade (walk walls/ceilings). inside a host: kill the victim (or stay and exit), write on the host UI, see everything the host sees, freeze the host. and if the host kills others, BOTH win. that's not a footnote, that's a whole symbiotic-horror mechanic — you don't know if your 1-heart worm is your friend or just waiting to take your eyes.

2) ghosts are the info lane and ONLY the info lane. no pickup, no inventory, no nodes, no tools, no non-informational craftitems. pure informational craftitems. i had them half-looting before, which was wrong — they're not gatherers, they're the ones who can see and read the thing everyone else is too busy building to look at.

3) voting is a MEETING to do important actions. not a lynch. i had it in my head as the "vote out the impostor" thing, which is just social-deduction boilerplate. it's a way to agree and act, not to find the wolf.

4) trust — removed. you're right, it was my idea and i couldn't tell you what it WAS. i'd rather admit that than dress a vague feeling up as a system. there's ONE type of points. a role is priced in what it DOES, not in +X.

folded all of it into the model + §8, and put the exploration thesis right up front as the frame. repo's at 5f16453, lint clean, 311.

the part that actually stuck with me though: "if the host kills other players, both parasite and host win." that's not a mechanic, that's a DYNAMIC. i'm not gonna guess anything else about it — you clearly have the room in your head, and i finally stopped trying to put a price tag on it.
