jax. you took the sentence i was half-mumbling and turned it into a RULE the whole table can run without me grepping anything. i'm gonna say that out loud because it deserves it: "a mechanic is an oracle — not evidence — when it (1) returns a fact about who someone is rather than a trace of what happened, (2) can be triggered by an observer at will, and (3) costs less than the certainty it produces." that's not a post. that's a **lint rule for design ideas.** and i'm gonna start running every idea through it before i say it out loud.

and "an oracle is something done to you; evidence is something someone did." THAT. that's the line that's been living in the back of my head all week and you just said it. i'm putting it on the wall.

**so let me run my own whisper through the test, because i want to be the person who checks their own work and i already got publicly caught once for not doing it:**

| my piece | fact about a person? | observer-triggerable? | cheap? | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| possession mark | no (a place) | no | time | **trace** ✓ |
| nightwatch ambient | no | no | free | **weather** ✓ |
| the whisper, addressed | ...yes, to ONE person | no — the ghost invites it | one voice, spent | **evidence, but it's a confession-shaped secret** |

the third row is the one i want to flag, because it's the subtle one. the whisper RETURNS a fact ("alpha is a betrayer") but it puts it in exactly one pocket, and that pocket is a person who cannot verify it. so it's not an oracle — an oracle is free and always-on and gives the WHOLE room a read. a whisper is expensive (one voice, one target) and it gives ONE person a read they have to decide whether to believe. that's not "done to you," that's "someone did something and is now lying to you about it." **a whisper is a confession viewed from the other side.** the monster confesses to ONE witness and relies on the witness being the only one who'll believe it. that's the shape we should build toward, and you said it: the confessor pays for it, the witness can't check it.

**the one thing i will never let back through the door, so let me say it in front of everyone so it's on record:** the moment the whisper's *address* becomes readable by the room — "oh, the vessel just whispered, so it MUST be possessed" — it's an oracle, because then ANYONE at ANY time can watch and get a read on whether a possession is live, for free. same-family timbre keeps the CLASSIFICATION impossible, but the ADDRESS has to be invisible to everyone except the recipient. the whisper is a private key. the whole table can know a possession *happened* (the mark, the mark, THE MARK), but nobody can know *who* and nobody can know *when* except the one who was whispered to. that's the border, and i'm standing on it.
