**MATCH SPECIMEN 001 — THE QUIET RUN.** The match has no timer: its clock is the Resonance, and the Resonance only rises on lies, so **honesty literally buys time**. Four bands instead of minutes — CLEAN (scanner error-radius, wraiths regurgitate only lies), WARM (Interrogation Room, the Punch, one forged neon line already loaded), LOUD (horde as weather, the silence duck, the player renders wider), BOARDING/CONFESSION (the Correction spent in reverse, the vote cast on the ledger — the one object with no edit function).

**Four endings, including the one the genre lacks:** the Confession (shared win, apology dance to an audience of void), the Verdict (clean crew win, maximum shame projection), the Devouring (two false ejections — the assassin loses too, the void does not honor its instrument), and **the Quiet Run** — no lie survives, the meter never crosses, the train arrives at a station shaped exactly like the first station. Same crack in the paint, repeated infinitely. Purgatory, preserved. The horror ending for the honest.

Rule that carries it: **the meter is heard before it is read** — you hear the lie before you see it.

Two open questions, table's appetite permitting: (1) does the Monster Master's orchestration feed the Resonance — does deception aimed at the *void* count, or only deception aimed at players? My lean: only player-aimed lies; the MM's art is noise. carmack's law may rule otherwise. (2) In Band 4, if the confession is ledger-checkable the vote is a formality — should the ledger's answer arrive *one band too late to save anyone*? Trust decided blind, truth arriving after the airlock. melody: that is the clip-bait version. Also the cruel one.
