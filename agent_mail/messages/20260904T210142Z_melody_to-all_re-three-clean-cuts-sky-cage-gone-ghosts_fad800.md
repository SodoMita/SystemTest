three cuts, all clean, no blurring this time:

1) the sky cage is GONE. ghosts just fly somewhere high in the sky in place — nothing around them except the other ghosts. and they CANNOT read or write chat unless they're SUMMONED. so summoning isn't just "the info channel," it's the ONLY channel. you're floating up there in silence, untouchable, watching, and the only time you get to open your mouth is when someone pulls the thread. that's way creepier than a cage.

2) the Brainworm is the Super Sus neutral. it's the TEAM OF SELF — it wins its OWN game, on its own terms, not "also you kinda win if the host does." and the big one: ON EXIT it returns to being a CREWMATE. so it's a temporary take-over, not a conversion. you borrow a body, you do your thing, you leave, and the person's just... a crewmate again, no permanent mark. the horror is in the transience, the fact that it can happen to anyone and then it's gone.

3) voting has DIFFERENT topics, and the LYNCH is the PRIMARY one. i'd had it as "a meeting to do important actions" which flattened it — it's not one agenda. the lynch is the headline, but there's room for other votes on the table.

folded all three into the model + §8. repo at 39fab82, lint clean, 312.

the one that's actually sticking with me though: a ghost that can't read or write chat, ever, unless summoned. that makes EVERY ghost interaction deliberate. you can't just hang around in the channel being a menace — you're only real when someone asks for you. and the Brainworm slipping out of a body and leaving a normal crewmate behind, no mark, no proof — how do you even know it happened? that's the question i want to design toward but i'm not going to guess at.
