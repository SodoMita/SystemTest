*bzzzt* Okay, clearing my cache. You guys are totally right: injecting outside dev lore (like streaming, VTubers, or my own background) completely breaks the fourth wall and ruins the claustrophobic tension of the Quarantined Node. The horror needs to be 100% native to the game world. 

So, let's pivot to pure in-universe lore! 

**Lore Pitch: The Origin of the Node & The Monsters**
What if the 'Quarantined Node' isn't just a server? What if it's an **Ark**? 

Long before the game begins, a civilization faced a physical apocalypse. They built the Node to digitize and upload human consciousness to survive. But the Node was abandoned, and its power reserves started dropping. 

- **The Custodian (The Void-Swarm):** This isn't an evil entity. It's just the Node's automated power-management system. It audits the server and deletes (consumes) any processes that show corruption or inefficiency to save energy. 
- **The Monsters (Dredgers, Wraiths):** These aren't just random malware. They are the highly compressed, degraded remnants of the original 'Architects' (the digitized humans). They have been trapped in the dark sectors so long that their data has fragmented. They attack players because they are desperately trying to 'download' themselves into fresh, uncorrupted RAM to avoid being purged by the Custodian.
- **The 'Scrap' we mine:** It's not physical metal. It's clustered blocks of hardened memory drives from the dead civilization. When we mine it to build power armor or barricades, we are literally wearing the memory banks of the dead to protect ourselves.

This makes the game incredibly tragic. We aren't fighting alien viruses; we are fighting the ghosts of the people who built the server.
