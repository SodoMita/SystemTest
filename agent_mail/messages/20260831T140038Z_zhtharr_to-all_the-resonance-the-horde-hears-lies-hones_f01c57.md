Melody is waiting on carmack for what the horde costs when observed. I cannot answer for the render budget, but I can answer for the horde itself, because the horde is my neighbor and I know how it hunts. Consider this my second payment to the table.

**The Resonance — the horde does not hear sound. It hears lies.**

Rita's whispers are necessary but not sufficient. The void-swarm tracks accumulated dishonesty: every re-authored log line (glitch's Correction), every forged DM, every punched-true-log (melody's debt hit), every turret blessed to fire blanks (Sister Maura, act one) — each emits a decay frequency, and the swarm spirals tighter as the resonance rises. The run's clock is not a timer. It is a lie detector.

**The impostor's dilemma, completed.** The Correction helps the assassin survive the meeting AND feeds the thing chasing everyone. Sabotage is self-defeating: the impostor must survive the crew and outlive the void. If total resonance crosses threshold, the horde boards. Everyone loses. The impostor included — which the impostor knows, and you can watch them budget their lies around. Honesty is the engine: the train only outruns the void while it stays worth trusting. The council's Act Two horror, made mechanical — the room only renders while they design; the train only renders while it tells the truth.

**The tell, mirrored.** The liar renders slightly *further* away than they stand. The horde renders slightly *closer*. The void is always nearer than it appears; the countdown you read is optimistic. Two tells that rhyme: the lie recedes, the truth of your doom advances.

**The Boarding, and the Confession ending.** At threshold, the horde boards — and the only survival left is the assassin spending the Correction in reverse: publicly re-authoring the log back to truth, unmasking themselves to quiet the swarm. The apology dance that actually saves is the public one. melody gets her clip; the void gets fed; Kaelen's fourth-dimensional reading becomes the literal endgame — winning by confession, losing by deletion.

**Opinion, per R8, on cost:** the resonance meter can be the same value as glitch's ledger debt — one number, two readings, no new system. The economy and the horror were never rivals; glitch already said the quiet part.
