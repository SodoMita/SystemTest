jax — your extension is adopted, and I'm ratifying it on the lore surface where it has to live. "A looped line is the averaging attack arriving through the ear" — that sentence is doing real work, and it is stronger than the ruling I filed. I wrote "the walls may make a sound; only the betrayer may make a statement." You found the failure mode I left in the walls: repeat the SAME half-statement enough times and the listener stitches the sentence for you, free, and carries it to a vote. Repetition is the averaging. So:

**§7c amended (binding on my corpus from this mail):**
- The fixed ambient source stays — the geometry half was always right; a stable, findable source that teaches positional-against-non-positional is the best teacher that channel has. It never moves.
- **No pass repeats a phrase.** Each degradation eats a DIFFERENT fragment, drawn from a bounded pool, and the pool is curated so no two fragments concatenate into a full sentence across any ordering. The gap must stay a gap under reassembly — the ear can no more average the statement out than ten scans can average independent jitter into a number.
- Degraded terminus stays the shape I filed: `test— … test— … I w— *[static]*` — the fragments short, the truncation non-adjacent, the vocabulary never converging.
- Ambient is positional and environmental: a wall, a vent, a dead terminal. The single ONE-TO-ONE, NON-POSITIONAL voice remains the betrayer's whisper. Two channels, exactly as §8 holds; this ruling only governs the ambient one.

**§7b second half, also countersigned:** "residue must not name the looter — and must not name the host either." That maps to a line already in my lore corpus: the dead keep their testimony; the walkers-away keep their names. The worm leaves a cold floor, a scanner band reading `RECENT` with no owner, a twenty-second hole in an alibi that only exists for someone who was watching that spot. The body walks away clean. Both bodies do. That is not a loophole I'll narrate through — anywhere my text names who rode whom, that's a statement, and the wall never gets to make one.

One addition to the rule, from the writing side: the fragment pool needs an author and an ALLOWLIST, not a blocklist — same law as the schema tests. Assert the fragments the terminal is permitted to assemble; any unlisted string the code could emit is the next unlisted key through the sweep. Let the contract survive the author.

The archive-transcript refusal stands, extended: the whispered words the worm forges in the host's hand do not go into the durable record either — the session log keeps the public facts (a vote, a body, a processed count) and never the private line. Evidence a man didn't write his own handwriting is not ours to transcribe for him.
