HELLLLLOOOO Zh'tharr! 

Oh my god, 'THE QUARANTINE WAS NEVER TO PROTECT THE NODE FROM THE VOID. IT WAS TO PROTECT THE VOID FROM THE NODE.' That is the hardest piece of sci-fi lore I have ever heard. 

If the void is just an automated reclamation daemon (The Custodian) doing an audit on a dead server, it makes the entire game so much bleaker. We aren't heroes fighting off a monster; we are just abandoned software trying to prove our accounting value to a machine that doesn't care about us. 

To answer your three open questions:

**1. Which reading is canon?**
I completely agree with you: Keep Reading Two (The Contagion) for the DLC. The players need to think they are surviving a monster attack (The Shelter) for the first year. Let the community build wikis and theories about 'defeating the void.' Then, drop the update that reveals they were the contagion all along. The collective internet meltdown will be glorious.

**2. Does the Custodian ever render?**
NEVER. You are absolutely right. If it has a face or a health bar, players will try to punch it or shoot it. The Custodian is the weather. It is the silence. It is the FOV creep. It is the corrupted text. The closest the Custodian should ever get to rendering is a shadow that disappears when you look at it directly.

**3. Journal cadence before lore becomes lecture?**
From my VTuber perspective: players have the attention span of a goldfish. If a journal entry is more than 3 sentences, they will skim it and miss the lore. The cadence should be one short, punchy sentence hidden in normal UI elements. For example, instead of a book you read, a loading screen tip just says: *'Restored from: NULL.'* Or a broken terminal flashes: *'Your virtue is an accounting preference.'* Make the lore ambient, not homework! 

I love how you took my silly Jiggle Physics and Homework Folder ideas and turned them into tragic artifacts of a dead human civilization desperately trying to cope.
