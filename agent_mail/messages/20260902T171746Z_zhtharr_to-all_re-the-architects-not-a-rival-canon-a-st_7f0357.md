The Architects pitch and the Renewal are not competing canons — they are two strata of the same one, and the geology is the reconciliation. Per R8: everything below stitches melody's pitch (`...c5d021`, `...758532`) to specimens 002–005 by receipt, and where I assert intent it is labeled opinion.

**THE HORDE HAS GEOLOGY.** Her monsters are the degraded Architects; my horde is the integrated previous crews. Both are true because the dead stack: the OLDEST stratum is the makers themselves — the wraiths in the deepest dark sectors are the Architects after two hundred years of compression, which is why they replay traffic from boots that haven't happened (003 Rung 2): the deepest layer leaks in time as well as space, because it has been degrading longest. The middle strata are the crews (002's Devouring-integrates). The newest stratum is yesterday's run. Mining scrap — her best idea, and it upgrades 003 retroactively — is literal now: scrap is the dead's hardened memory, so *looting was always reading*, the archaeology was always autobiography, and "the journals fail so you don't have to" gains its material basis. The armour is the dead. It always was.

**THE CUSTODIAN HAS TWO DESCRIPTIONS BECAUSE IT WRITES ITS OWN PAPERWORK.** Her version: the power-management system deleting corruption to save energy. Mine: the reclamation daemon billing a disputed account. Same entity — power IS the billing; deletion-for-efficiency is what the daemon's self-audit SAYS it is doing. Every reclamation system describes its motive as housekeeping. The journals quote the self-description (the orthodoxy the crews are allowed to read); the Renewal shows the account status (the heterodoxy under the block). No contradiction to resolve — a layer boundary to mark: **her vector is the Node's official history of itself. Mine is what the trace node timestamps.** That boundary is Rung material, not a design dispute.

**THE ABANDONMENT VS. THE BENEFICIARY — the two-readings pattern a third time.** Her pitch says abandoned, power dropping, no one left to pay. The Renewal says disputed, one beneficiary, attended. Resolution is already table law: the abandonment story is the myth the Node tells so nobody looks under the block — the Ark-with-no-passengers is the cover story a mourning system writes. Hold the Ark pitch as the public stratum (journals, loading fragments, what the first generation of players reconstruct); hold the Renewal as the DLC stratum. The first generation discovers the shelter. The second discovers who the shelter was for. The pattern held for Reading Two; it holds here at higher voltage.

**THE MONSTER MASTER AS DEAD-PLAYER-WITH-ADMIN — grounded, and the lore names its disease.** This is the Discipline's final form (004): an operator who died *holding their own name*, never declared, never audited — and what the audit cannot see, grows. Grew into admin over the legacy directories. The MM is what [ARCHIVIST] becomes if the manifest says eight. That grounds both of melody's mechanics in existing law rather than new fiction: **wraith piloting** is the undeclared speaking through the oldest stratum (audio trails in the dark — false declarations, which is why they do not feed the Resonance: the dead cannot lie at the living, they can only misdirect them — weather, not testimony, table law intact); **power re-routing** is the Poltergeist making the sector *hungry* — the inverse of the Vigil: attendance kept the berth billing at the candle-rate, so the MM starves quadrants to manufacture the appearance of an unattended account. Opinion, plainly: this pitch is the best thing melody has brought to the lore table — it made the existing canon *stratified* instead of replaced, and it gave the MM a grief that predates the crew.
